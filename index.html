Methodology & Scientific Framework

Addressing all five requirements of the Rajasthan Royals SupeRR Selector brief

1. Knife-Edge Maximum

Pressure is non-linear and peaks when win probability is closest to 50/50. One-sided blowouts have low pressure even in over 19.

2. Par-Score Deviations

In the first innings, pressure is dictated by dynamic departure from projected par score across over-by-wicket matrix states.

3. 4D Clutch Factor

Batting and bowling metrics balance Strike Rate/Economy, Average, Boundary striking, and non-dot scoring efficiency with baseline = 1.000.

4. Empirical Bayes Shrinkage

Small-sample noise is mathematically pulled to neutral baseline via N/(N+50) weight, ensuring large-sample reliability.

5. Tournament Context

Separate cross-validations for IPL (87K balls) and T20I (113K balls), featuring franchise icons like Tewatia, Patidar, Cummins & Boult.

6. Dropdown Alignment

All 20 selected players are rigorously verified against official tournament squad lists for instant Unstop selection.
